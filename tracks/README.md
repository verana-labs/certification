# Role tracks

A track is the set of modules a role needs. Every track includes the three core modules (C1, C2, C4). Completing all modules of a track under one program version earns the Level 2 certification named after the track, except Fundamentals, which earns Level 1 with a single online exam.

| Track | Modules beyond the core | Typical candidate | Launch phase |
|---|---|---|---|
| Fundamentals (Level 1) | none | Anyone | 1 |
| Developer and Integrator | T2, T6, and one of T3 or T1 | Engineers building on VS Agent or wallets | 1 |
| Infrastructure Engineer | T1, T5, T6 | Platform and operations engineers | 1 |
| Solutions Consultant | C3, B1, B5, B6 | Architects and consultants designing deployments | 1 |
| Pre-sales | B2, reduced B1 | Sales engineers and partner managers | 1 |
| Ecosystem Operator | C3, T4, B6 | Staff of a governing body or registry operator | 3 |
| Legal and Compliance | C3, B4 | Counsel, data protection and compliance officers | 3 |
| Procurement and Tenders | B3, reduced B4 | Buyers and bid teams | 3 |
| Wallet Provider | T3, T6 | Personal and business wallet teams | 3 |
| AI Agent Builder | T7, reduced T2 | Teams shipping agents on Verana | 4 |

**Reduced** means the module's material is required reading and its exam questions enter the track exam, but the module's practical assessment is not required.

A module passed under a program version counts for every track that includes it under the same program version (Certification Policy, Section 4.2).

The machine-readable definition is `program.json` at the root of this repository.
