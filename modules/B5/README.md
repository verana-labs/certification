# B5. Economics

> **Outline.** The material of this module is being written by the Showcase and Certification Working Group. The objectives, outline and assessment below are the agreed scope for program version 2026.1.

4 class hours. Assessed by: exam.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can:

1. Explain the native coin, trust units and fiat pricing, and exchange rates.
2. Compute fee flows and deposit growth along a participant tree for a given volume.
3. Price an ecosystem: fees that cover costs, deposits participants can bear, zero-fee public-good schemas.
4. Explain what the Foundation, the Council and the network each earn and spend.

## Prerequisites

C2, C3.

## Outline

### 1. Units

The native coin; trust units and the governance-owned exchange rate; fiat obligations; why deposits are always in the coin.

### 2. Flows

Beneficiaries along the tree; the deposit share; wallet and user-agent rewards; worked examples with the indexer's beneficiaries preview.

### 3. Pricing an ecosystem

Cost model; volume scenarios; sensitivity to coin price; when to price in TU or fiat.

### 4. Who earns what

Validators, ecosystems, issuers, wallets; the Foundation's role; what is not a revenue.

## Assessment

Knowledge exam (sixty minutes, seventy percent) with calculation questions.

## Reading list

VPR specification v4: Business models; Trust deposits; Exchange rates. docs.verana.io: Understand, Ecosystems, The business model; Build, Ecosystems, Design the fee model.
