# C3. Ecosystem governance

> **Outline.** The material of this module is being written by the Showcase and Certification Working Group. The objectives, outline and assessment below are the agreed scope for program version 2026.1.

4 class hours. Assessed by: exam and case.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can:

1. Explain what an ecosystem governance framework must contain and how its digest on the registry binds participants.
2. Design the roles and onboarding modes of an ecosystem from its policy goals.
3. Set fees along a participant tree and predict who pays whom.
4. Explain revocation, ending an accreditation, and slashing, and when a governance framework uses each.
5. Estimate the running economics of an ecosystem: fees in, deposits, operating costs.

## Prerequisites

C1, C2.

## Outline

### 1. Governance frameworks

What an EGF is for; the mandatory sections (purpose, eligibility, onboarding evidence, fees, revocation, slashing, disputes, versions); the digest on chain; the ECS ecosystem's framework as an example; the Verana Certification Ecosystem's policy as a worked case.

### 2. Roles and modes as policy

Translating a policy goal (who may issue, who may verify, whether holders are tracked) into onboarding modes, grantors and validity periods; when grantors are worth it.

### 3. Fees along the tree

Validation, issuance and verification fees; discounts; pricing assets; the trust deposit share on every fee; preview of beneficiaries on the indexer.

### 4. Revocation and slashing

Ending versus revoking an accreditation; revoking credentials versus revoking issuers; ecosystem slashing of a participant's deposit; network governance slashing; evidence and process.

### 5. Economics of an ecosystem

Costs (operations, onboarding, support), revenue (fees), deposits as locked capital; sizing fees; the public-good case for zero-fee schemas.

## Assessment

Knowledge exam (sixty minutes, seventy percent) and a case: given a two-page description of a sector, write the roles, modes, fees and slashing rules of its ecosystem and justify them.

## Reading list

VPR specification v4: Ecosystems; Credential schemas and participants; Trust deposits; Business models. docs.verana.io: Understand, Ecosystems. The Ecosystem governance framework template of the Verana Council.
