# B4. Legal and compliance

> **Outline.** The material of this module is being written by the Showcase and Certification Working Group. The objectives, outline and assessment below are the agreed scope for program version 2026.1.

6 class hours. Assessed by: exam and case.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can:

1. Explain how a governance framework allocates liability among ecosystem, validators, issuers, verifiers and holders.
2. Assess a deployment against GDPR: data minimization, what is on chain, what is personal data, roles of controller and processor.
3. Position Verifiable Trust against eIDAS 2: qualified and non-qualified attestations, trusted lists, wallets, the gaps.
4. Explain the licensing of the specifications and software, and the token considerations an organization must take into account.

## Prerequisites

C1, C2, C3.

## Outline

### 1. Governance frameworks as contracts

Binding participants; the digest as evidence; versioning; dispute clauses; slashing as liquidated damages.

### 2. GDPR

Nothing personal on chain; digests and pseudonymity; wallets as data subjects' tools; controller and processor mapping for issuers, verifiers and the registry; retention.

### 3. eIDAS 2

QEAA, EAA, PuB-EAA; trusted lists versus ecosystems; wallet requirements; third countries; where Verana complements and where it does not apply.

### 4. Licensing

CC BY-SA for specifications; Apache 2.0 for software; what a contributor signs; trademark policy.

### 5. Tokens

The native coin as a utility; fees and deposits as costs, not investments; accounting and tax questions to ask counsel; sanctions and travel-rule touchpoints.

## Assessment

Knowledge exam and a case: a GDPR and liability review of a deployment described by the certificator.

## Reading list

Verana Council governance framework templates. GDPR articles 4, 5, 25, 26, 28. eIDAS 2 regulation (EU 2024/1183). Foundation legal pages.
