# I1. Instructor module

> **Outline.** The material of this module is being written by the Showcase and Certification Working Group. The objectives, outline and assessment below are the agreed scope for program version 2026.1.

4 class hours. Assessed by: observed session.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can:

1. Deliver the official material of a module without altering objectives, assessment or pass criteria, and mark your own additions.
2. Proctor a knowledge exam under the proctoring guidelines and document incidents.
3. Grade practical work and case studies against the Foundation's rubrics, consistently and with a written rationale.
4. Keep candidate records and report results to the Certification Office on time.

## Prerequisites

Level 2 in the track to be taught.

## Outline

### 1. The material

Structure of a module; timing; labs and environments; the test network's quirks; what you may add and how to mark it.

### 2. Proctoring

Identity checks, environment, recording, incidents, the integrity rule and its consequences.

### 3. Grading

Reading a rubric; anchoring examples; second marking; writing a rationale a candidate can learn from; appeals.

### 4. Records and reporting

What to keep, for how long; reporting within five business days; the platform's certificator pages.

### 5. Conduct

Conflicts of interest; the Code of Conduct; confidentiality of banks, rubrics and solutions.

## Assessment

Observed session: the candidate instructor delivers one module section and grades one anonymized lab, observed by the Certification Office.

## Reading list

The Certification Policy, Sections 4.5, 5 and 10. The proctoring guidelines and rubrics (private repository, access through your certificator).
