# B2. Demonstration and pre-sales

> **Outline.** The material of this module is being written by the Showcase and Certification Working Group. The objectives, outline and assessment below are the agreed scope for program version 2026.1.

4 class hours. Assessed by: live demo.

## Learning objectives

After this module you can:

1. Deliver the playground story live in twenty minutes to a mixed audience, with a wallet in hand.
2. Answer the ten common objections with facts from the specifications.
3. Position Verana against DNS-style trust lists, national wallets, enterprise identity products and other trust registries.
4. Qualify an opportunity: who the ecosystem is, who issues, who verifies, who pays.

## Prerequisites

C1, C2, C4, and the reduced B1 reading.

## Outline

### 1. The story

The problem in one screen; a wallet, a service, a verdict; one use case end to end; the registry view; what the audience should remember.

### 2. Demo craft

Preparing a playground run; what can fail and the fallback; narrating verdicts; never improvising a claim.

### 3. Objections

Why a blockchain; who runs it; what about eIDAS and national wallets; what about X.509 and trusted lists; privacy; cost; agents.

### 4. Positioning

Complementary versus competing; the layer argument; the public-good argument; when not to pitch.

### 5. Qualification

The four questions of an opportunity; mapping to designations and tracks; handing over to solution design.

## Assessment

Live demonstration to a certificator panel, graded by rubric: story, correctness, handling of two objections.

## Reading list

The Foundation's positioning notes. Playground: all use cases. Module C1 and C2 key takeaways.
